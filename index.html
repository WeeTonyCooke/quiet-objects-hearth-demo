The room

Open fires, proper food, and a hundred thousand welcomes.

A neighbourhood bar and restaurant on Malin Road — stone floors, old wood, and a room that doesn’t hurry you. Come for pizza and a pint; stay when the fire’s in.

A proper pint, by the fire.

Guinness on draft, soft drinks, and whatever’s pouring at the bar.
